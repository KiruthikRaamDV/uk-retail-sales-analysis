# UK Retail Sales Analysis

## Overview
A data analysis project exploring two years of real transaction data 
from a UK-based online retailer. The goal was to move beyond surface-level 
numbers and understand the patterns behind what sells, who buys, and when 
— the kind of insight that actually helps a business make better decisions.

## What I Analysed
- Sales trends broken down by month, day of week, and hour of day
- Best performing products by both revenue and volume sold
- Which countries generate the most sales outside the UK
- Customer segmentation using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis
- Revenue forecasting using a 3-month moving average

## Why RFM Analysis
Rather than treating all customers the same, RFM analysis segments them 
by behaviour — how recently they bought, how often they buy, and how much 
they spend. This gives a retailer a practical starting point for targeting 
the right customers with the right message, rather than a blanket approach.

## Tech Stack
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Plotly, Seaborn, Matplotlib

## Dataset
Online Retail Dataset from Kaggle — real transaction records from a 
UK retailer spanning 2009 to 2011.

## How to Run
1. Open the notebook in Google Colab
2. Download the dataset from Kaggle
3. Upload it when the notebook prompts you
4. Run all cells from top to bottom
